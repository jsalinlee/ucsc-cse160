Notes for Grader:
Rubric criteria notes -
-- Basic scene -- 
    - (3 primitives): Half cylinder chimney, and the little candies are actually a mix of icosahedrons and dodecahedrons.
    - Candies spin
    - Dim directional light from above

-- Primary shape texture: The chimney is textured and the candies are actually textured with an alphamap to make it look like a crystal

-- Custom textured 3D model: The soot sprites and the furnace

-- Camera movement: Basic mouse Three.js Orbit controls

-- Hemisphere lighting, diffuse lighting, and point light coming from fireplace

-- Skybox: 360 projection

-- 20 shapes: Yes

-- Extra feature: Spirited Away! Just kidding. I didn't have enough time to customize, but I wanted to have the candies actually roll around. And I was also planning a sequence where the soot sprites "toss" coal into fire, chase down a candy, and eat it.
